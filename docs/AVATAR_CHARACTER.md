# A character for every avatar (DRAFT, for the owner's approval)

The owner's ask from the start: each avatar has its own nature, and its tap animation shows it ("one crosses arms, one
smiles, one grumpy face, each with its own nature"). Today all animations do the same smile, tilt and blink. This sheet
gives each of the 30 a nature and **one distinct 2 s gesture**. **Nothing is generated until the owner approves it**;
the cap then comes from the lead's held budget (D). Revised after the lead's review.

Contact sheet for the phone: `docs/mock/avatars.html` (the two framing options, then the 30 stills with the nature and
gesture under each, in Hebrew).

## Rules

- **No nature follows from gender, skin, glasses, size or clothes.** "Shy" is the hedgehog's.
- **No two natures and no two gestures alike.** Each gesture is one big movement of the whole upper body or of a clear
  feature (shell, propeller, flower, screen, visor, bubbles), so it reads at tile size (about 96 px). No face-only moves
  except the fox's wink, which is kept.
- **Body part × rhythm:** no cell holds two characters, no body part more than two (except the head, three), and the two
  sharing a part move in clearly different rhythms (table below).
- Kind: nothing mocking or scary; the grumpy one is funny-grumpy, never mean. Safe everywhere: no thumbs-up, no OK sign.
- As before: no teeth, no tongue, no distortion, no outfit change, same style and framing, static camera; ends on the
  still. Every frame is checked before use.
- Avoided because they fail: a turned cap, a hand at a cap brim, a fast salute, many octopus arms, wings opening, a hand
  over the face.

## The owner's decision

**Option 1 for all 30 (recommended): head and shoulders, as the approved portraits.** Upper-body moves only; the
portraits he approved stay. The grumpy one reads without arms: shoulders up, head turned away with an "hmph", back with a
small smile. Cost: 29 new animations (the fox's is kept) × 3.0 = **87.0**, with retakes (about one in five) **at most
about 105**; at a cap of **90** the hardest fall back to the still with the CSS bob.

**Option 2 as a test first: waist up with hands, on three characters only** (boy-cap crossing his arms, girl-ponytail's
fist pump, raccoon's "shh"). Three waist-up stills (3 × 0.5, one redo: 2.0) and three animations (3 × 3.0, one or two
retakes: 12 to 15): **at most 15**. If the hands come out well and the stills match the approved portraits, he can then
decide whether more characters get the waist-up version. No decision on all 30 before that test.
